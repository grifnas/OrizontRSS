ORIGINAL ASSET — Orizont RSS

Concept: a warm sun rising above a white horizon, with three clear RSS waves.
The dark blue rounded background provides strong contrast on light and dark Windows themes.
The icon contains no text and is intentionally reduced to a few geometric shapes so it
remains recognizable from 16×16 pixels through 256×256 pixels.

Palette:
- background: #123A6D
- sun: #FFC857
- horizon and RSS waves: #FFFFFF

The distributable OrizontRSS.ico is generated from this design in multiple raster sizes.
